Why local matters

Nearly every transcription service works by upload: your recording goes to someone else's server and comes back as text. Fine for a casual chat. Not fine when the meeting covered salaries, an acquisition, a customer list, or an unreleased spec — and at many companies, policy simply forbids it.

MeetingNotes Studio runs recognition on your Mac, using Apple silicon and either Breeze-ASR-26 or Whisper MLX depending on the language. Audio and transcription never leave the machine. Text is sent out only if you press AI Summary and pick an online model — a step you take deliberately, and can skip entirely by choosing an on-device model.

What it does

Offline recognition, Taiwanese included

Breeze-ASR-26 or Whisper MLX is selected by language. Breeze is trained for Taiwanese contexts, so Taiwanese and mixed Mandarin-English meetings come through.

Record, import, or capture YouTube

Record the room, import M4A, MP3, MP4 and other common audio or video files, or transcribe system audio while YouTube plays in your browser.

Timestamped transcripts

Every segment maps back to a moment in the audio. Click the text, hear that second. Proofreading stops being a guessing game.

AI summaries with decisions and to-dos

Summarize after you have checked the transcript. Pull out key points, decisions and action items — with an on-device model, or an online one using your own API key.

Export into real documents

Word (DOCX), PDF, plain text, and a format that imports cleanly into Google Docs. Nothing is trapped inside the app.

Minimal permissions

Microphone access for recording; screen and system-audio capture only if you turn it on for YouTube transcription. Neither runs in the background on its own.

Questions

Does it really handle Taiwanese?

Yes. Taiwanese runs through Breeze-ASR-26, a model trained for Taiwanese contexts. It handles Taiwanese and code-switched Mandarin-English-Taiwanese meetings better than general-purpose models — the case most online services still handle poorly.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Recording and recognition both finish on your Mac. The only thing that can leave is transcript text, and only when you press AI Summary with an online model selected. Choose an on-device model and nothing touches the network.

Do I need an internet connection or an API key?

Neither, for transcription — no account required. An API key is needed only if you want summaries from an online model; on-device summaries need nothing.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple silicon Macs. Offline recognition relies on Apple silicon's machine-learning performance, so Intel Macs are not supported.

Can I transcribe lectures or YouTube videos?

Yes. Capture system audio while the video plays in your default browser, or import the file directly — MP4 and other common formats are supported.
